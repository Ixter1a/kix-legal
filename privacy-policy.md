# Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 23, 2026

This is a draft for your Discord app, not legal advice. Keep it accurate to what ] ・KIX・ [ actually does.

## Who handles data

] ・KIX・ [ is operated by its owner (zeroixprojects@gmail.com).

## What data is used

] ・KIX・ [ sends Discord the messages its owner chooses to publish: text, Components V2 layout, links, and attachments the owner adds.

The bot token and webhook URLs stay on the owner’s computer. They are not published.

] ・KIX・ [ does not read or store other server members’ conversations, member lists, or Discord profile details.

## Who receives data

The message the owner chooses to send is transmitted to Discord, because Discord delivers that message to the server. This data is not sold and is not sent to a separate third-party database.

## How long data is kept

] ・KIX・ [ does not keep its own archive of messages. A sent message remains in Discord under Discord’s rules until someone deletes it.
